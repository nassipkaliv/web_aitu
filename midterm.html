My trip to Da Nang

Vietnam

We spent a few days in Da Nang. Most of the time we walked along the Han River, looked at the Dragon Bridge and went to the beach in the evenings.

On weekends the bridge breathes real fire and water, so a lot of people come to watch it.

City from above

Da Nang from a high floor. The city is much bigger than I thought.

Fire show

At night the dragon breathes fire. It's loud and you feel the heat even from the crowd.

Sunset at the beach

The beach in the evening. Warm water and almost no people.
